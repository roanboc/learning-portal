What do you want to learn?

Two collections you can explore in your browser. See how data moves through an organisation, or watch life organise itself in a simulated world. Each one stands on its own, so start wherever you like.

Films · Hands-on labs · Scenarios

Learning Data

Short films, labs and real-world scenarios that show how a modern data platform works, from the moment data is captured to the decision it informs, and why its meaning matters on the way.

Topics: data platforms, data modelling, analytics engineering, data quality, enterprise architecture, and master and reference data.

Start with the first film →

Simulations · Guided labs · Runs in your browser

Learning Systems

Simulations of one world, Gaia, seen at different levels of complexity, from chemistry and cells to bodies, nervous systems, ecosystems, the planet and societies, and at key moments in the history of life.

Every stop is a live simulation, and guided labs let you change one rule and see its effect at another level.
